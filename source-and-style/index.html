Discrimination and Basketball

Black Youths Playing Basketball at Stateway Gardens

This photograph shows Black youths playing basketball at Stateway Gardens, a public housing project on Chicago's South Side, in May 1973. The photograph was taken by photographer John H. White and is part of a collection from the National Archives.

This object connects to my project about discrimination in basketball because it shows basketball as part of everyday life in a Black Chicago community. Cultural images like this can help show how basketball is connected to race, community, and social conditions.

Object Information

  • Photographer: John H. White
  • Date: May 1973
  • Location: Chicago, Illinois
  • Collection: National Archives
  • Topic: Basketball and Black Chicago communities
